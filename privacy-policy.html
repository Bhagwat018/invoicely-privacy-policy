This Privacy Policy explains how Invoicely - Invoice & Billing Manager (the "App"), developed and operated by Shivyn App Studios ("we", "us", or "our"), handles your information when you use the App on your Android device. By downloading or using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

Summary: Invoicely is an offline-first invoice and billing management tool. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data, business records, financial totals, or client information to external servers. All your data (invoices, client profiles, business settings, bank details, and generated PDF files) is stored locally on your device only.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect, store, or share any personal or financial information such as your name, business name, client details, email address, phone number, GSTIN/Tax ID, or bank account numbers. The App operates offline and does not require an account, login, or registration.

2. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

The App stores the following information only on your device using your device's secure local storage. This data never leaves your device and is never transmitted to us or any third party:

  • Your business profile information (business name, email, phone, physical address, GSTIN/Tax ID, bank details, and signature).
  • Your client directory (client names, email addresses, phone numbers, billing addresses, and tax identifiers).
  • Your created proforma and tax invoices, including line items, prices, tax rates, and notes.
  • Generated PDF invoice files created for previewing or sharing.
  • App preferences such as theme selection (dark/light mode) and onboarding status.

You can delete or reset this data at any time by clearing the App's data in Android Settings or uninstalling the App from your device.

3. Permissions We Use

The App requests minimal permissions solely to perform its core functionality — generating, saving, and sharing PDF invoices:

  • Storage / Media Access (READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE, WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE, READ_MEDIA_DOCUMENTS): Used to save generated PDF invoices to your device and allow you to view or share them through external apps like Email or WhatsApp.

The App never reads or accesses your private files, photos, or messages outside of the PDF documents generated by the App itself.

4. Third-Party Services & Advertising

The App operates offline for invoice calculations and PDF creation using open-source, embedded JavaScript PDF compilers. It does not send your invoice data to any remote cloud API or PDF rendering server.

5. Data Security

Since we do not collect or store any personal data on remote servers, there is no remote server data that can be compromised. All local data resides on your physical device, protected by Android's device-level security and sandbox architecture. We recommend keeping your device's operating system updated and securing your phone with a passcode or biometric lock.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is a general-audience business utility application and is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will assist in clearing any local data.
